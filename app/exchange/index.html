What we actually know

Prices are public executions, not current buy/sell order-book depth. The OSRS Wiki v2 feed supplies current high and low transactions, five-minute and hourly price/volume aggregates, plus item metadata and GE limits. All markets can be scanned in a few bulk requests.

Sale-side tax is 2%, rounded down and capped at 5,000,000 GP per item. Officially exempt items are excluded from taxation. Four-hour item buying limits and eight member slots (three F2P) are used in the planner.

What's deliberately missing

The public API cannot supply your account balance or offers. Enter your budget and completed fills manually. Only sales matched to earlier recorded acquisition lots count as realised earnings. Tax is based on your actual total when supplied, otherwise current rules are an assumption.

The first version has no trading automation. It does not buy, sell, cancel or alter any offers.
