Privacy and your local records

Draft for private QA, 14 November 2026. Publisher/support contact and public hosting URL must be supplied before store submission.

The Golden Exchange keeps your trading journal, tickets, settings and watchlist on your device. It does not ask for game passwords, connect to your character, place offers, or send your journal to our servers. We operate no app backend, analytics or advertising service.

Market requests

Market data comes directly from prices.runescape.wiki over HTTPS. The provider receives normal network information such as your IP address and request headers. Opening an item's historical chart sends its item ID. We do not control that provider's logging or retention. Search text and personal trade records are not sent with market requests. External links open their respective websites, whose privacy practices apply.

Backups and deletion

Exported JSON contains your local records. Android's share sheet transfers it only to a destination you choose, which may have its own privacy policy. Keep backups private. Import replaces or merges records only as indicated by the confirmation. The Journal’s Delete local records button clears app records, caches and retained migration originals after confirmation. Shared/exported copies stay where you saved them. Uninstalling or clearing app data also deletes local records; export a backup first.

Release status

This privacy text documents current QA behaviour. Final Data Safety declarations, provider retention review, publisher contact and hosting are unresolved submission gates. There is no claim of Google Play or Jagex approval.
